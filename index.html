ABOUT PARAS4N6

Researching the digital traces we leave behind.

PARAS4N6 is an independent technical research space focused on digital forensics, artifact research, investigative strategy, and tool development.

The goal is to document technical observations through testing, reproducible methodology, and careful interpretation of the underlying evidence while considering user behavior and tedencies.

Views and content therein do not represent that of the author's employer(s), but rather, serves as a respository to gather thoughts and general findings to amplify personal research interests and deep-dive methodologies.
